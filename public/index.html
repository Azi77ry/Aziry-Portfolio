Will my WhatsApp number get banned?

The agent replies at a human pace with typing delays, keeps daily sending limits and honours customers who opt out. These are the habits WhatsApp expects from a normal business account. No tool can promise zero risk, but these lower it a lot.

Does the agent speak Swahili?

Yes. It answers in Swahili or English, depending on the language your customer writes in.

Can you build a custom system for my business?

Yes. I build Python and Django backends, Next.js and Node.js web apps, and M-Pesa, Airtel Money and HaloPesa payment integrations for businesses in Tanzania and East Africa.

How long does setup take?

A WhatsApp agent is usually live within one to three days after you send your product list. Custom web projects are scoped and quoted individually.
